About the founder

A practical automation partner, not just another tool installer.

I'm Qasim Shaikh, founder of FutureTech AI and a CRM and business automation specialist. I focus on the systems behind growth: how a lead enters your business, where its details are stored, who follows up, and what happens next.

My work brings together CRM setup and workflow design, lead routing and follow-up, WhatsApp automation, Google Sheets integrations, and AI-assisted processes. I aim to make the process easier to understand, easier to manage, and less dependent on repetitive manual work.
